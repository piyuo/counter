Piyuo Counter helps you understand foot traffic and vehicle activity with privacy-first, on-device AI.

Place a phone or camera where you want to monitor. Piyuo Counter detects people and vehicles, then creates anonymous traffic summaries including pass-bys, entries, exits, occupancy, and average stay time.

Privacy comes first. All AI processing happens on your device. Videos, images, and audio never leave your device. Piyuo Counter does not identify people or use facial recognition — it only analyzes movement patterns.

Features

• AI-powered pedestrian and vehicle counting
• Anonymous traffic insights with 5-minute summaries
• Works without internet connection
• Local Device Only mode with no uploads
• Designed for continuous 24/7 monitoring

Perfect for retail stores, offices, restaurants, exhibitions, and public spaces.
